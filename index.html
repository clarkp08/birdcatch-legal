What is BirdCatch?

BirdCatch gets you outside, moving, and truly observing the natural world around you. It builds real knowledge of bird species, habitats, and conservation status while honing your photography and birding skills.

Your BirdDex becomes a personal record of every species you have ever caught and every place you have ever birded. Connect with a global community of BirdCatchers, compete on leaderboards, and contribute real data to conservation efforts through the EarthDex.

Whether you are a casual player or a dedicated birder, BirdCatch grows with you and the world grows a little more documented with every catch.

Go catch 'em in the wild.

How it works

01

Photograph a bird

Find a bird in the wild and take its picture. Any bird, anywhere in the world.

02

Get your catch graded

Your photo is identified and scored on species rarity, location, season, and photo quality.

03

Build your BirdDex

Every catch joins your field guide. Climb the leaderboards and claim First Ever catches.
